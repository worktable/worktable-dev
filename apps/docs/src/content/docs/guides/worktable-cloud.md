Worktable Cloud hosts your workspace. Open it in a browser or in
[Worktable Desktop](/start/desktop/). Content uses the same portable format as
local and self-hosted Worktable.

To run Worktable on your own computer or server instead, use
[Desktop](/start/desktop/) or the [CLI installer](/start/install/).

## Open a workspace

1. Open [Worktable Cloud](https://app.worktable.cloud/) and sign in.
2. Choose a Worktable or create one. Each Worktable has its own subscription.
3. Open your Worktable and complete onboarding. In Desktop, choose the
   Worktable after signing in; Desktop remembers it.

Invite people from **Settings → Team**:

- Only an account with the invited email address can accept.
- Choose a starting Space to open for them when they join.
- If the email doesn't arrive, copy the invitation link and send it yourself.

New Spaces are shared by default. Owners can access every Space, and Space
creators manage their Spaces. Threads follow the access of their Space.

## Connect an agent

Open **Settings → Agents** and choose your client. Supported clients authorize
through a browser sign-in. See [Connections](/start/connect-your-agent/) for
client instructions, skills, and verification.

Then complete your [first project](/start/first-space/).

## Manage your workspace

- [Sharing](/guides/sharing/): public document links.
- [Workspace backups](/guides/import-export/): hosted backups, exports,
  restores, and moving to another installation.
- [Cloud account](/guides/cloud-account/): billing, cancellation, sign-out, and
  the export window after paid access ends.

Worktable Link connects a local or self-hosted installation through your Cloud
account. Its content stays on its own device. See
[Remote access](/guides/remote-access/#worktable-link).

## Usage analytics

Usage analytics is on by default. Turn it off in **Settings → Privacy**. It covers your connected agents too and never includes your content. These docs don't use cookies. See the [privacy policy](https://www.worktable.cloud/privacy).
