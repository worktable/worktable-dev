Start with one project brief. To use the prompts below,
[connect an agent](/start/connect-your-agent/) first.

## Setup and Getting Started

A new Worktable starts with a short setup: name it, connect your agent, and
invite your team or skip that for now. Setup ends in the **Getting Started**
Space, which stays at the top of the sidebar until you remove it.

Its home lists your first steps, and each one ticks off when it happens:

- **Connect your agent.**
- **Add Worktable to your agent's global rules.** Copy the line it gives you
  into the rules your agent reads in every chat, so you don't have to say "in
  Worktable" each time.
- **Set up your first Space.** Copy a prompt, such as one that sets up a Space
  for your current project.
- **Invite your team** (owners only, can be skipped).

Each person has their own list.

## Create a brief

Use an existing Space or create one from the sidebar. From the Space's **+**
menu, choose **Doc**. Write the objective, constraints, and the next question to
answer.

Or give your agent the source material and ask:

```text
Find or create a Space called Community workshop in Worktable.
Create a project brief from these notes: a two-hour repair workshop for
20 people, one main room, and four volunteers. We have not chosen a date.
Separate confirmed details from open questions. Keep the brief durable.
```

Replace the example details with your own. Ask the agent for a link to the
result.

## Keep it accessible

Docs created in the app start temporary. To keep the brief, open **Archives**
and choose **Keep**. Then choose **Pin** from the document menu.

Click the Space name to open Space Home. The brief appears under **Pinned**.

## Request a revision

Select a passage in the brief and leave an annotation, such as:

```text
Add a checklist of questions to resolve before choosing a date.
Do not assume volunteer availability.
```

Ask the agent to address the open annotation, then review its change and
reply. An annotation alone does not start an agent session.

You can also edit the document yourself. Open its history to compare or
restore a previous version.

## Continue later

In a new session, ask your agent to find the Community workshop Space, read
the brief, and summarize the open questions. It doesn't need the earlier chat.
