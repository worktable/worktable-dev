Open **Settings → Billing** to see your subscription or choose **Manage
billing**. Checkout and the billing portal show the current price, taxes, and
renewal terms.

Each Cloud Worktable has its own subscription, and billing stays with the
payer. Becoming an owner does not grant billing access. To manage billing after
leaving a team, open **Your subscriptions** from **Your Worktables**.

## Cancel or recover

- Cancelling stops renewal at the end of your paid period.
- A failed renewal payment has a seven-day grace period. Payment retries do not
  extend it.
- When paid access or the grace period ends, the workspace becomes export-only
  for 30 days. A full refund also starts this window. You can still manage
  billing, sign out, or download an export.
- Reactivate before the 30 days end, or the workspace is deleted.

Download an [export](/guides/import-export/) while you still have access.
Content on linked local or self-hosted devices stays on those devices. See the
[Cloud terms](https://www.worktable.cloud/terms).

## Sign out

Choose **Sign Out** in your profile menu to end the Cloud session in this
browser. It does not delete the workspace or disconnect your agents. Your
identity provider may still be signed in.

Desktop has its own sign-out controls; see [Desktop](/start/desktop/).
Signing out does not unlink a linked local device. Pause or unlink it as
described in [Remote access](/guides/remote-access/#worktable-link).

## Get help

For billing or refunds, email
[support@worktable.dev](mailto:support@worktable.dev) from your account address.
The [Cloud terms](https://www.worktable.cloud/terms) explain refund eligibility.

For account deletion or a privacy request, follow the
[privacy policy](https://www.worktable.cloud/privacy). Exporting your workspace
does not delete the hosted copy.
