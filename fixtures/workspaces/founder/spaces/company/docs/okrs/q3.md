# Q3 operating plan

The `okrs` collection is the live source for status. This document holds the reasoning and tradeoffs behind the numbers.

## Quarter thesis

Prove that teams return because Worktable becomes part of how they operate, not because a single demo is impressive. Revenue, activation, reliability, enterprise readiness, hiring, and customer learning are one system this quarter.

## Decisions for this week

1. Keep the activation team focused on the first shared artifact, not broader onboarding polish.
2. Ship audit-log export before adding more SSO providers.
3. Close the product-engineer offer before opening another role.

## Risks

- Enterprise work can consume the product roadmap if design partners do not share a common need.
- Activation experiments are moving the metric, but the sample is still small.
- Founder time is split across sales and late-stage hiring.

## Review rhythm

Owners update records by Thursday. Leadership resolves changes in [the weekly review](/leadership/weekly-review) on Friday, then the dashboard becomes the Monday operating view.
