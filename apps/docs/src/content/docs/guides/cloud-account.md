Open **Settings → Account** to see your subscription status or choose
**Manage billing**. Checkout and the billing portal show the current price,
taxes, and renewal terms.

## Cancel or recover

Cancellation stops future renewals and takes effect at the end of your paid
period. A failed renewal payment has a seven-day grace period for normal access;
payment retries do not extend it.

When paid access or the grace period ends, the workspace becomes export-only
for 30 days. During that window you can manage billing, sign out, or download a
complete workspace export. Reactivate before the window ends to keep the
workspace; otherwise it is deleted. A full refund also starts the 30-day export
window. These rules are described in the
[Cloud terms](https://www.worktable.cloud/terms).

Download an [export](/guides/import-export/) while access is available. Content
on linked local or self-hosted devices remains on those devices when the Cloud
subscription ends.

## Sign out

Choose **Sign out** in **Settings → Account** to end the Cloud session in this
browser. It does not delete the workspace or revoke agents' separate access.
Your identity provider may still have an active session. Choose **Sign in
again** to return.

Desktop has its own sign-out controls; see [Desktop](/start/desktop/).
To stop access to a linked local device, pause or unlink it as described in
[Remote access](/guides/remote-access/#worktable-link). Signing out alone does not
unlink it.

## Get help

For billing or refund help, email
[support@worktable.dev](mailto:support@worktable.dev) from your account address.
The [Cloud terms](https://www.worktable.cloud/terms) explain refund eligibility.

For account deletion or a privacy request, follow the
[privacy policy](https://www.worktable.cloud/privacy). Exporting your workspace
does not delete the hosted copy.
