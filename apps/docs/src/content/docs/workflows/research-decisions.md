---
title: Research decisions
description: Compare supplied evidence, record a decision, and update it as new information arrives.
---

Use a document when you need to compare evidence and explain a decision. This
example chooses a venue for a fictional community workshop.

## Supply the evidence

Give your connected agent these fictional proposals:

| Venue | Hire cost | Capacity | Confirmed access | Equipment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cedar Hall | $180 | 24 | Step-free | Tables included |
| River Room | $120 | 18 | Step-free | Tables included |
| Orchard Studio | $160 | 25 | Unconfirmed | Table hire costs $40 |

The workshop needs room for 20 people, step-free access, and tables. The venue
budget is $200. Ask:

```text
Use the supplied fictional proposals to create a durable Venue decision
document in the Community workshop Space. Reuse that document if it exists.
Compare the options against our requirements. Cite each proposal by name,
include equipment in the total, and distinguish confirmed details from
questions. Do not invent missing facts or contact anyone.
```

## Review the result

Open the document. Check that Cedar Hall costs $180, River Room does not meet
capacity, and Orchard Studio costs $200 with access still unconfirmed. The
comparison should explain why an unresolved requirement affects the choice.

Annotate the recommendation:

```text
Separate the preferred option from a confirmed booking. List what we
must confirm before committing.
```

Ask the agent to address that annotation. Review its revision, then pin the
document to **Start here** in the Space.

## Update the evidence

In a later session, provide a revised fictional Cedar Hall proposal: hire cost
is now $220, with all other details unchanged. Ask the agent to find the existing
decision, update the affected comparison, and explain whether its recommendation
changes.

The revision should show Cedar Hall over budget, keep Orchard's access question
open, and avoid presenting any option as satisfying all confirmed requirements.
Use document history to compare the change.

For a large set of sources that need separate fields and updates, consider a
[records collection](/guides/records/). Three proposals do not require one.
