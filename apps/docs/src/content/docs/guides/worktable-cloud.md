Worktable Cloud hosts and maintains your workspace. Open it in a browser or
connect through [Worktable Desktop](/start/desktop/). Your content uses the same
portable format as local and self-hosted Worktable.

## Open a workspace

1. Open [Worktable Cloud](https://app.worktable.cloud/) and sign in.
2. Choose a Worktable or create one. Each Worktable has its own subscription.
   Checkout shows the current
   price and terms before purchase.
3. Open your Worktable and complete its onboarding. In Desktop, choose the
   Worktable after signing in; Desktop remembers that destination.

Cloud is optional. To run Worktable on your own computer or server instead,
use [Desktop](/start/desktop/) or the [CLI installer](/start/install/).

Invite people from **Settings → Team**. Each invitation is emailed and can be
accepted only by an account with that email address, personal or on another
domain. Choose a starting Space to open it for them when they join. If the
email doesn't arrive, copy the invitation link and send it yourself. The link
names the Worktable and who sent it; accepting creates an account with the
invited address, or signs in to an existing one, and opens the Worktable. Newly
created Spaces are shared by default; owners can access every Space and Space
creators manage their Spaces. Threads follow the access rules of their
containing Space.

## Connect an agent

Open **Settings → Agents** and choose the setup for your client. Supported
clients authorize through a browser sign-in flow. Follow
[Connections](/start/connect-your-agent/) for client instructions, optional skills,
and verification.

Then complete your [first project](/start/first-space/).

## Manage your workspace

- [Sharing](/guides/sharing/) creates and revokes public document links.
- [Workspace backups](/guides/import-export/) covers hosted backups, exports,
  restoration, and moving content to another installation.
- [Cloud account](/guides/cloud-account/) covers billing, cancellation,
  sign-out, and the export window after paid access ends.

Worktable Link connects an existing local or self-hosted installation through
your Cloud account where available. It keeps that installation's content on its
own device. See [Remote access](/guides/remote-access/#worktable-link).

## Choose whether to share analytics

Optional usage analytics is off until you opt in. Open **Settings →
Privacy** to change the choice for your Cloud account and connected agents.
Analytics never includes your documents, record values, searches, or agent
instructions. Local, self-hosted, and linked-device content activity does not
send product analytics.

The marketing site and these docs have their own analytics controls in the
footer. See the [privacy policy](https://www.worktable.cloud/privacy) for details.
