title: Spaces
description: Organize projects, find content, and manage temporary and durable documents.
---

A Space groups related work, such as a project, a client, or an ongoing area.
Start with one and add structure as the work grows.

## Create and organize

Create a Space from the sidebar. Its **+** menu creates documents, drawings, and
folders. Keep names descriptive and paths shallow.

- Drag documents and folders to reorder them.
- **Rename** changes a doc's title and file name, or an HTML doc's path, such as
  `research/interviews`. Links and bookmarks keep working.
- Folder actions apply to everything inside, so check the contents before
  archiving or deleting a folder.

Search before creating a second document on the same subject, so its history
stays in one place.

## Pinned docs

Click a Space name to open Space Home: pinned docs, recent docs, temporary docs
with their archive dates, and the Space's activity.

Choose **Pin** from a document's menu for entry points such as the project brief
or current plan. Pinned docs also appear at the top of the Space in the sidebar.

## Document lifetimes

- **Durable** documents stay active until someone archives them.
- **Temporary** documents sit apart from the main sidebar tree and archive seven
  days after their last edit, rename, or comment. You can pick another date.

New documents start temporary. Clear **Start as Temporary** in the Space's
**+** menu to change that. Tell agents whether to keep what they create.

On a temporary document, open the **Archives** chip under its title to
**Keep** it, change the date, or **Archive now**. A durable document's menu
offers **Make Temporary**.

Archiving hides a document without deleting it. Restoring makes it durable.
Archived documents are never deleted automatically.

## Find content

- **Search** covers documents and records across Spaces, and can include
  archived results. Drawings match on typed text only.
- **Home** shows what is pending for you, recent durable docs, and temporary
  docs that archive soonest. Space Home does the same for one Space.
- **Activity** lists what people and agents did, newest first. Filter by Space,
  by you, or by agent.
- **Threads** lists conversations across Worktable; each Space has its own.

Under a doc's title, a line shows who edited it last. Open it for recent
activity and **Version History**. The count beside it shows how many docs,
records, and HTML Docs link here; open it to see them.

For the different content formats, see the [overview](/#choose-content).
